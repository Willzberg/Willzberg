How do I know a part fits my car?

Send us your make, model and year, and the part name or number. We confirm the fit before you pay. For engine-specific parts, a photo of your old part helps.

How do I pay?

We confirm the final price first, then you pay by MTN MoMo to +256 779808319 or in cash. Prices on this site are indicative.

Do you deliver?

Delivery can be arranged when you order. We confirm the cost and timing with you, or you can collect from Buwate, Kira-Kasangati Road.

Can you fit the part for me?

Yes. Book a service and we can fit parts from our catalogue at the workshop, or send a mobile mechanic to you.

What if the part I need is not listed?

Message us the part name and your vehicle details and we will source it for you.

Where are you based?

Buwate, Kira-Kasangati Road. Call +256 704137708 before you visit so we can have your part ready.
